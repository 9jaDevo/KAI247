# P7 commercial hypothesis — unvalidated

KAI247's initial customer is a Nigerian small-business owner/operator personally handling hosting, vehicle documents, permits and service agreements. A second interview group is the IT provider already helping those owners manage domains/hosting. Multi-client organizations, billing and payments remain outside this MVP.

The hypothesis is that customers with enough scattered documents will pay to keep a source-backed deadline, the owner's explicit approval, replacement-date history and auditable email attempts together. A calendar can already provide inexpensive reminders; KAI247 must earn its place by reducing repeated document checking and preserving why a date was chosen. We have not measured time saved, prevented losses or demand. A customer with only a few stable dates may reasonably prefer their calendar.

**Price experiment proposed for founder review:** free five-item email-only hypothesis from the master; test **NGN 2,500 per owner/month** for a proposed 25-item plan. This is an interview/landing-page research hypothesis, not a launched plan, enforced quota, payment capability, purchase or demonstrated willingness to pay. No discount, sale or recurring billing is implemented. Costs and real usage must be understood before promising a sustainable price.

## Short interview (about 10 minutes)

First ask permission: “May I take notes? May we anonymously summarize your feedback in our project write-up? May we quote any particular sentence? You can decline any of these.” Do not record business documents, customers or private identifiers without separate consent.

1. Tell me about the last renewal you handled. Where was its date and how did you check it?
2. Which documents or dates are hardest to keep straight? What happens when the date is ambiguous?
3. Show how you currently use a calendar/spreadsheet. What works well enough already?
4. After this synthetic demo, what would stop you trusting an extracted date or reminder?
5. Would the source quote and replacement history change your workflow? Which step would you still check yourself?
6. What would you need to see before uploading a real document? Are hosted AI processing and retention acceptable?
7. If offered at NGN 2,500/month, would you trial it, decline or need a different plan? Why? This is research, not a payment request.
8. What single change would make you use this next month? May we follow up, and may we use an anonymous summary of today's feedback?
